## Q1. Which other US banks matter, and what are the US bank stablecoin consortium efforts?

### Takeaway
The most important US addition is the bank-owned **Early Warning Services / Zelle "ZelleUSD" (ZLUSD)** stablecoin, announced in June 2026. Seven big banks own it, and two of them, Capital One and Truist, are not yet tracked. **SoFi Bank** (SoFiUSD on Ethereum/Solana) and **PNC** (bitcoin trading via Coinbase) are the most active individual US banks not yet tracked. Crypto-banking specialists **Cross River**, **Customers Bank** and **Lead Bank** sit behind many stablecoin and fintech announcements. Several of the names also carry heavy collision risk from stadiums and place names.

## Q2. Which European banks matter, and what are the euro-stablecoin bank consortia?

### Takeaway
**Qivalis** is the pan-European bank consortium for a MiCA euro stablecoin. It is Amsterdam-based, has applied to DNB for an EMI licence, and targets H2-2026. It grew from about ten founders to **37 banks** by 2026-05-20, and it is the single most important European addition. Among individual banks, the following stand out:
- **BBVA**: retail BTC/ETH trading, Ripple custody technology and Agorá.
- **Lloyds**: tokenized sterling deposits on the Canton Network, the GBTD pilot, Agorá and Fnality.
- **AMINA Bank**: the first bank to support RLUSD, plus Ripple Payments.
- **ING**: Qivalis, Fnality and crypto ETPs in Germany.
- **DZ Bank**: meinKrypto with BTC/ETH/LTC/ADA.
- **KBC**: Bolero crypto trading and Fnality.
- **Raiffeisen Bank International**: Bitpanda across 11 markets.
- **Intesa Sanpaolo**: BTC/ETH/XRP ETF holdings.

## Q5. Bank-led networks: Partior, Fnality, Canton participants, Project Agorá, mBridge, RLN, Kinexys partners

### Takeaway
**Project Agorá** should be added as an entity. It processed real money in July 2026 and is the single network that connects most of the candidate banks across Europe, Japan, Korea and Latin America. **Partior** (DBS/JPM/SC/DB/Emirates NBD) and **Fnality** (a sterling wholesale payment system with 24 bank and FMI shareholders) are Recommended. **mBridge** is mainly a central-bank project with no named commercial banks, so it is Optional.
