## Q1. Which other US issuers matter for crypto ETFs/ETPs, and which have active filings or products in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
Beyond the issuers already tracked, the US names that keep showing up next to top tokens in 2025-2026 are **ProShares, Volatility Shares, REX-Osprey (REX Shares + Osprey Funds) and its T-REX line with Tuttle Capital, Teucrium, and Amplify ETFs**. These are mostly first-to-market futures, leveraged, staking or income products on XRP, SOL, DOGE, ETH, LINK, ADA, XLM, AVAX and SUI. **T. Rowe Price** (TKNZ, launched 2026-07-16) and **Morgan Stanley Investment Management** (Bitcoin and Solana trusts, filed 2026-01-06; Solana trust ticker MSOL, with staking) are the first large traditional managers to launch in-house spot crypto ETFs, so they rank highest. Cyber Hornet and Yorkville America/Truth Social are niche filers. No crypto ETF from Schwab Asset Management was found; Schwab's 2026 crypto activity is in brokerage and spot trading.

### Inferences
- In 2025, the fastest-moving US issuers on top-125 altcoins were the small specialists: Volatility Shares, REX-Osprey, Teucrium, Tuttle and ProShares. They were often "first" on XRP, SOL, DOGE, LINK, ADA, XLM, AVAX and SUI, so they come up often in token-specific ETF news. They belong in the Must or Recommended tiers.
- T. Rowe Price and Morgan Stanley matter for a different reason. A large traditional manager launching its own spot crypto product is the kind of "institution → project" event the tracker records. Morgan Stanley's SOL staking structure also names Figment, Galaxy and Coinbase.
- Schwab is better handled under the broker/bank category (other researchers), unless Schwab Asset Management later files an ETF.

## Q2. Which non-US issuers matter (Europe, Hong Kong, Japan, Korea, plus Canada and Brazil)?

### Takeaway
- **Europe.** The traditional managers with real 2025-2026 crypto activity are **Amundi** (tokenized MMF share class on Ethereum, 2025-11; SAFO with Spiko on Ethereum and Stellar, 2026-03), **DWS** (AllUnity/EURAU euro stablecoin with Galaxy and Flow Traders, live 2025-07-31) and **Janus Henderson** (Anemoy/Centrifuge funds, see Q3). Among ETP specialists, **Valour (DeFi Technologies)** is the most active with altcoin ETPs (SUI, SOL, XRP), including listings on Brazil's B3.
- **Hong Kong.** **China Asset Management (Hong Kong)** stands out: spot BTC and ETH ETFs (older, 2024), ETH staking (2025-05), Asia's first spot SOL ETF (2025-10-27), and tokenized MMFs in HKD, USD and RMB. **Bosera** tokenized its money-market ETFs with HashKey.
- **Japan.** SBI Global Asset Management, Nomura, Daiwa, MUFG, Amova and Asset Management One are preparing crypto trusts and ETFs ahead of FSA reform (2026) and possible TSE listings (around 2027, unverified).
- **Korea.** **Mirae Asset** took over Korbit (renamed Digital X, 2026-07) and is tokenizing Global X ETFs with Ondo. Samsung Asset Management runs HK-listed bitcoin futures and blockchain ETFs. KB, Shinhan, Hanwha and Korea Investment are preparing for spot ETFs. The government plans to amend the Capital Markets Act in the second half of 2026.
